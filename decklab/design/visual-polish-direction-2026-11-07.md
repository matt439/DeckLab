# Deck Lab: visual diagnosis and proposed direction

Date: 7 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane. Owner: `E11-S30`. Status: **direction with a first production slice implemented; wider rollout and acceptance remain**.

The [first implementation and rendered comparisons](visual-polish-implementation-2026-11-07.md) record shared controls, Testing composition, receipt guidance/footer and editor recovery/reflow. The original study below remains historical proposal evidence; it does not describe the final production markup.

Matt's clarification is the brief: the current UI looks amateurish, and he wants us to identify why and resolve it. He does not need to identify the individual CSS problems or prescribe a style. The review must supply a defensible visual diagnosis, an actual proposed treatment and a coherent implementation plan.

The [rendered change plan](rendered-ui-change-plan-2026-11-07.md) retains functional findings, typography/spacing criteria and acceptance details. Those are useful foundations, but a list of bugs and checks is not a visual direction. This document replaces that plan's earlier assumption that the current visual system should be preserved unchanged.

## Diagnosis

**Too many things carry the same visual importance.** The app has a consistent set of tokens, but its rules produce repeated bold violet capsules, outlined actions, tinted regions and explanatory blocks. As a result, the interface looks assembled from components rather than composed around the user's current task. This is a design judgment supported by the examples below, not a numerical measure of professionalism.

| Cause | Visible example and consequence | Design correction |
|---|---|---|
| **Action, navigation, selection and status look too similar.** | On the [Testing board](rendered-ui-change-plan-2026-11-07/board-1440-light.png), current navigation, view switches, format switches and row actions all use prominent rounded violet treatments. On the [receipt](rendered-ui-change-plan-2026-11-07/receipt-430-coarse-light.png), Everything arrived, Tools, filter selection, ticks and Record arrival all demand attention. The eye keeps finding controls rather than a clear next task. | Establish visibly different levels: one filled lead action where a natural lead exists; neutral secondary controls; quiet utilities; understated navigation/selection. Keep focus visible and meaningful statuses explicit. |
| **The same pill silhouette is overused.** | Buttons, navigation, segmented groups, steppers and status tags have much the same rounded outline. The receipt's wrapped grey filter group becomes a large floating shape. The visual grammar gives a tiny tag and a major action similar character. | Use modest corners for ordinary controls, plain tab treatments for page views, contained segments only for short choices, and compact pills/tags for status/counts. Painted shape and 44px touch hit area are separate decisions. |
| **Typography spends emphasis too freely.** | Headings, buttons, filter labels, names and chips all have strong weight. Supporting instructions occupy several similarly prominent lines. The page has many local headings without a clear overall reading order. | Retain the readable font families; use regular prose, medium control labels, semibold item names and selective strong headings. Put a key number beside its explanation. Remove repetition before reducing font size. |
| **Some containers and introductions outweigh their contents.** | The earlier [deck Overview](rendered-ui-review-2026-11-07/11-how-to-play-lands-overview-top-1440-light.png) stacks a tall hero, tinted metadata band, readiness panel, tabs and a full-width empty play-history panel before substantive content. The [library detail](rendered-ui-review-2026-11-07/10-library-how-to-play-1440-light.png) places stats, prose and cards on one large mint wash. These treatments consume attention even when little information needs it. | Use a compact identity/header area, concise actionable readiness, inline empty-history metadata, neutral work surfaces and small card/deck identity accents. Keep the cards as the source of character. Verify populated hero/history states before setting final dimensions. |
| **Rows and task areas lack a stable composition.** | Current Testing results align right while historical results move below left-hand prose. The editor gives the name the remaining width after controls, producing fragments in the [enlarged-text sample](rendered-ui-change-plan-2026-11-07/editor-root200-320-light.png). Related facts and actions do not always share a predictable position. | Define stable identity, evidence and action areas; keep historical evidence in the same scan position with an explicit historical label. Give card identity priority and move tools below it when constrained. Align header, toolbar and rows to one grid. |
| **Space is distributed by components rather than importance.** | Receipt instructions/filters and its bottom bar crowd checking work. The editor's empty Considering section has a heading, explanation, empty message and separate action while editing controls compete in a wrapped footer. Wide layouts can be sparse while individual rows are cramped. | Remove empty-state ceremony and repeat guidance. Compose bars into deliberate summary/action rows. Preserve reading width, grouping, touch separation and clear decision space; do not simply reduce every margin or fill every blank area. |

The receipt's outer violet ring in the captured state includes genuine focus styling. It is not evidence of two redundant permanent selection borders. Keep the independent keyboard focus ring; review the permanent checkbox, central decorative tick, dimming and selection treatment separately. Also exclude placeholder-art quality, fixture-only repeated prose, single-deck fixture emptiness and mid-image fixed bars in full-page captures from the diagnosis.

## Proposed direction

**A restrained card workbench:** card/deck imagery supplies personality; neutral surfaces, aligned information and quiet tools supply structure. Violet remains the action/selection accent, with fewer large competing applications. This continues the useful phone/desktop distinction without assuming that desktop polish means smaller text or that phone friendliness requires every control to be a large pill.

These are starting choices to prove in rendered comparisons, not universal design rules:

- **Type:** keep Figtree for UI/prose and JetBrains Mono for evidence where numerical alignment helps. Compare roughly 28px page titles, 18–20px section headings, 16px phone body and 14px desktop body; supporting text stays readable. Use existing semantic tokens or deliberately revise their shared values. Prefer 400 prose, 500–600 labels/names and selective 600–700 headings. Keep long guidance out of italics; retain semantic rules reminder text. Inline links remain recognizable, including the existing card-inspection distinction.
- **Controls:** ordinary buttons/fields start with 6–8px corners. A neutral border and ink label distinguish a secondary action; utilities can be unboxed. Reserve filled violet for a clear lead action and selected-state cues that need it. Page-view tabs get a quiet baseline/selection marker rather than another large capsule. Do not remove touch area, hover, disabled/busy meaning, focus visibility or accessible names.
- **Surfaces:** use the page ground, a work surface and raised overlays deliberately. Prefer spacing and subtle dividers for ordinary groups. Avoid nested cards for every paragraph or statistic, and broad deck-colour tints behind unrelated content. Keep modest identity stripes/pips and art.
- **Information:** show the name, important state/number and relevant action first. Context stays beside the fact it qualifies. General teaching copy can move to an accessible disclosure; blockers, unknowns, current scope and review consequences stay visible. Preserve the exact existing data and safety semantics.
- **Space:** keep bounded reading columns and clear section separation. Reclaim space from duplicate introductions, empty panels, arbitrary heights and oversized wrapping chrome. Judge a gap by whether it groups, separates, aligns, stabilizes loading or protects an interaction; its emptiness is not itself a defect.

Changing to a different font or accent colour alone will leave most of these causes intact. Conversely, a neutral rectangle is not automatically more polished: the entire page's hierarchy and composition must support it.

## Rendered direction study

A disposable study renders the actual app DOM at **1440×900 Testing**, **393×700 Testing** and **430×700 Receive delivery**, in both themes at DPR 2. Each case compares the current page, a quieter CSS treatment, and a composition proposal. The comparison uses the same synthetic records, checked state and top-of-page scroll position. It changes no production source, data, saved draft or running service.

The quiet-only treatment reduces violet outlines and capsules, distinguishes navigation from actions, and groups the results. It does **not** solve task composition: restoring the receipt's 44px recovery target actually makes its uncomposed footer taller. The next treatment therefore moves secondary order/context and general checking help into native disclosures, removes repeated visible shop framing, groups progress/recovery and date/action deliberately, and shortens Testing's default scope explanation while retaining the full explanation. The scope still states Commander **1v1**, sample size and that the win rate is not a rating. Pairing/precon are quiet alternatives on Popular; row actions retain their own scope.

The proposal retains original text/control nodes, placing some secondary text in disclosures. It adds a short visible count/scope; the original complete receipt heading remains its accessible heading in this study. This retention mechanism is for comparison, not a recommended final markup implementation. Native disclosures were opened and closed through their summary controls. No Save, Record arrival, Play or simulation request was activated. [Curated proof](visual-polish-direction-2026-11-07/study-proof.json) and the [image manifest](visual-polish-direction-2026-11-07/screenshot-manifest.json) retain the executed boundary and measurements. The proposal styles and DOM-regrouping function are retained beside them for inspection; the app does not load these files.

The final retained run completed six cases / eighteen captures with no page errors or document-wide overflow, unchanged application-source fingerprints and confirmed browser/fixture/runtime cleanup. Twelve current/composed PNGs are retained; the intermediate quiet-only measurements remain in the proof. At 430×700, both themes measured the receipt grid at **y=612px current**, **524px quiet-only** and **306px composed**; the bar began at 494.5px, 467.5px and 510px respectively. Thus the initial grid lies below the bar at the top-of-page position in the first two treatments; the composed version exposes about 204px of grid above it. The composed footer is 134px high versus the current 149.5px, while its recovery target grows from 24px to 44px. These measurements show allocation of this specific viewport, not recognition, scroll reachability, physical-touch acceptance or a general quality score.

**Design judgment:** this is a credible, calmer direction, with clearer action/navigation distinctions and more emphasis on working content. It is not the finished design. Historical Testing evidence still moves to a different column; the header/toolbar widths still need one alignment system; and the editor plus populated library/deck pages have not received this treatment. The short receipt still needs scrolled/selected-row and real-art comparisons, and the bulk action belongs nearer the board tools in the final composition. Type sizes are largely held constant in the study so shape, emphasis and layout changes can be assessed; the proposed type scale still needs its own comparison. Production-shaped explanatory copy, varying titles, busy/error states, accessibility and complete interactions remain to qualify.

|---|---|
| **Testing** | A compact title/scope header, attached Popular/Matchups/Rankings navigation, a nearby compact format choice, then results. Popular's exact per-deck actions lead; pairing is secondary there. Desktop rows keep identity/state, coverage, result/freshness and actions in stable areas for current and historical evidence. Phone rows stack that same order. |
| **Receive delivery** | Order context and one short checking instruction, quieter filters/tools, then a card-first board. Compare two phone picture columns. Consolidate general metadata guidance while keeping uncertainty and recovery available. Compose the footer as deliberate progress/date and action rows; do not hide blockers or change preview/apply. Simplify permanent selection decoration separately from keyboard focus. |
| **Editor** | Make names the first-class row content, with quantities and tools aligned beside them or on the next line. An empty Considering area becomes a compact heading/count and Add action, with help available on demand. Footer summary, problem state and Save have defined places; Undo/Discard are quieter and always reflect live draft state. |
| **Deck Overview** | Compact art/identity, title/status and actions; a concise actionable readiness strip; tabs and useful deck content. Empty play history is inline rather than an entire leading panel. Keep actual results and populated history valuable. |
| **Library and detail** | Preserve master/detail where it helps selection, with a neutral detail surface and small identity accents. Keep cover art and useful selected-row feedback. Test many decks before judging the empty area left by a one-deck fixture. |
| **Variant result** | A clear verdict with its scope and next action, followed by consistently aligned evidence. Exclusion reasons remain readable. Narrow group evidence uses vertical rows when necessary instead of squeezing a three-column table and scale labels. |

## Delivery order

1. **Prove the direction on real rendered components.** Compare current/proposed Testing and Receive delivery using the same synthetic content, viewport, theme and scroll position. Add the editor and populated deck/library states before locking the complete visual system. A CSS study can demonstrate quieter controls and spacing; it cannot by itself prove content regrouping, new disclosures or interaction behavior.
2. **Implement shared foundations together.** Revise secondary/quiet controls, control radii, navigation/segmented roles and the type hierarchy in their shared owners (`tokens.css`, `components.css`, `shell.css`, `base.css`, card components where needed). Update the P1 specification/UI kit to the resulting rules rather than scattering overrides across pages. Keep privacy, events, original controls and data contracts intact.
3. **Compose the hard task pages.** Testing first, then receipt and editor, using the page treatments above. Incorporate relevant recovery/readability fixes from the [repair lane](rendered-ui-change-plan-2026-11-07.md#implementation-order). Shared visual improvement and correctness can progress together; neither is a substitute for the other.
4. **Apply the same grammar to deck/library and the remaining pages.** Compare before/after with populated, empty, failed and busy states. Fix exceptions in the shared owner where they represent the same component. Preserve card identity and real domain distinctions.
5. **Validate the complete experience.** Check both themes, relevant phone/tablet/laptop/QHD sizes, long names, actual enlargement, focus/touch targets, keyboard flows and reduced motion. Keep real Safari, software keyboards, screen readers, real art and production state/durability acceptance explicit. A successfully captured screenshot is evidence to inspect, not automatic aesthetic acceptance.

The delivery should be judged by whether a viewer immediately knows what the page is about, can distinguish its important information and next action, and can scan related facts without competing decoration. Record the actual visual comparisons and state checks. Do not close the work merely because individual token values or regression tests pass.
