# Deck Lab journey maps

Dated 2026-10-13, rechecked against the code and [stories.md](stories.md) on 2026-10-19. Part of the design set: [README](README.md) (method and index), [personas](personas.md), journeys (this file), [user stories](stories.md) and the [gap analysis](gap-analysis.md).

## How to read these

There's one journey map for each epic, E1 to E11. Each map follows one or more [personas](personas.md) through one realistic scenario, from what sets it off to the point where they're done.

- **Stage** is a step in time. The stages run in order, but people skip or repeat some of them.
- **Doing** is what the person actually does.
- **Touchpoints** are the pages (`#/library`, the deck page's Cards tab), commands (`python -m decklab.db ...`) and physical things (the storage box, sleeves) they use at that step.
- **Thinking and feeling** is the question in their head and how the step feels. Anything said about User1, the members or the Commander table beyond the known facts is marked as an assumption.
- **Pain points** are what goes wrong or is missing today, checked against the code and [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) on 2026-10-13 and rechecked on 2026-10-19; a fixed one says so, with its story. "PLAN.md item N" means that item of PLAN.md's "Still to do".
- **Opportunities** are ideas, not decisions. The [user stories](stories.md) and the [gap analysis](gap-analysis.md) say which of them are worth doing. Where Matt has decided one (his answers of 2026-10-13, listed in [personas.md](personas.md#answered-2026-10-13)), the cell says so.

Where a cell holds more than one point, each starts with a bullet. Pokémon is out of scope throughout: it isn't part of Deck Lab.

Personas used below: Matt (admin), User1 (member, home space, on a phone; Matt, 2026-10-13), Claude Code (works for Matt at the PC), the members (User2, User3 and User4, and User5, the template for every member; each with their own private space, signing in only through decklab.example.com; occasional users at most, on phones), and the Commander table (the other players at a social Commander night). See [personas.md](personas.md).

**Scenario:** Matt has changed Izzet Otters, or is thinking of buying a card for it, and wants to know whether the deck is better before he spends money or sleeves it. **Where it stands:** stats, practice hands, goldfish, Forge matchups, the field rank, per-format Rankings (E3-S10, 2026-10-15), Test this change and the trust labels are built. Results, fields and rankings follow deck privacy (E10-S31): each viewer sees only results whose every side they may see. Sideboard-plan tests are built (item 6, E4-S16, 2026-10-29). ## E5. Decide what to buy

**Personas:** Matt, User1, Claude Code. **Stories:** [stories.md, E5](stories.md#e5-decide-what-to-buy).

**Scenario:** Izzet Otters (or Alela Faeries) is short of cards. Matt works out which missing cards are worth buying, checks Australian shops through Claude, tops the order up from the watchlist to share postage, and has the order recorded so the queue drops and the cards show as on the way. User1 adds cards she'd like to her wishlist along the way.

**Where it stands:** the pages show each deck's missing cards and converted price estimates with rate and date. Shopping now combines individual home-deck shortfalls into a canonical card list, names the wanting decks and matching queue rows, shows dated reference prices, and groups the queue By shop (E5-S04/E5-S07/E5-S09, built 2026-10-15). The deck price table sorts by Card, Missing and extended missing cost (E5-S22). These are research references, not live shop stock; the combined list is not a concurrent-build packing count. Reprint follow-ups are built (E5-S11, 2026-10-16): known upcoming paper reprints name their set/date with snapshot coverage, and waiting rows offer a manual price review after a listed release since the saved check. Queue editing, recording orders, cancellation with an explicit recorded-queue restoration choice and mistaken-order removal are built.
